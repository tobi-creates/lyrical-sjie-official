THE ROOTS

I was born and raised in Langa — Langa N2 7455, Cape Town. Not just from Langa, I'm Langa. Every corner, every Kasi, every wifi hotspot story.

Growing up, it was Emtee The Hustla on repeat. That hustler spirit — how he made vernac rap feel big. I knew I wanted to tell my own story too.

THE USB THAT CHANGED EVERYTHING

After primary school, a friend copied FL Studio to my USB stick. No tutorial, no YouTube data — just curiosity.

I taught myself how to make gqom beats. Kick, log drum, repetitive — but it hit. People started calling me DJ Sjie.

DJ Sjie was the producer era. Parties, local jams, making beats for fun. But deep down, hip-hop was always the way for me.

CLASSROOM CYPHERS

In class, I would write lyrics instead of notes. Pages full of bars. Sometimes I'd freestyle for the whole class — teacher looking confused, classmates hyped.

After school, KwasKom. My friends, a circle, a beatbox or a phone beat — and I would freestyle for hours. That's where Lyrical Sjie was born, not DJ Sjie.

THE INFLUENCE

J. Cole — for the storytelling, the honesty, the college-kid-who-made-it energy.
SimulationRxps — for the underground grind, keeping it raw, no industry polish.
Emtee — for showing a kasi kid can go national.

I'm all three in one: Langa soul, hip-hop heart, gqom bounce in my drums.
